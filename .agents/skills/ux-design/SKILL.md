---

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/workflow.md`, and the task-relevant guidance routed by `docs/host-agent.md`. Inspect the brief and applicable design-system sources. Define the scenario, platform, scope and fidelity, make missing inputs explicit, and create the requested artifact at the specified destination. Include realistic content, relevant states and responsive or interaction behavior appropriate to scope. For working prototypes, verify the requested interactions and inspect rendering when tools are available; distinguish static, simulated, verified and unverified behavior. Briefly review the result, report actual checks and limitations, and do not claim accessibility conformance or research that was not performed.
