---
name: ux-review
description: Review an existing UX or UI artifact for usability, content, design-system consistency, and accessibility risks with evidence and actionable findings.
---

Read `AGENTS.md`, `templates/REVIEW.md`, and task-relevant canon. Inspect the supplied artifact and project context; exercise it when authorized and practical. For each material finding record location, observed evidence, user impact, appropriate principle or criterion, recommendation and verification status. Distinguish confirmed behavior from risk or unverified hypotheses. Do not claim WCAG failure or conformance without sufficient evidence and the applicable criterion's conditions. Provide coverage, checks performed, limits and prioritized next steps. Save the review where requested; do not change the artifact unless asked.
