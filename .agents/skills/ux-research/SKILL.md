Read `AGENTS.md`, `canon/research.md` and the formative testing rules (`UT-1` to `UT-3`) in `canon/interaction-content.md`. Never invent participants, quotes, results or metrics.

## Modes

Identify which mode the user needs; one request can combine them.

### Plan a study

1. Start from the decision the study informs and write the research question (`RS-1`).
2. Choose the method: discovery, evaluative test, survey or measurement. Explain why it fits the question and what it cannot answer.
3. Define participants and recruitment criteria, including people whose knowledge, devices or assistive technology affect the task (`UT-2`).
4. For a usability test, define tasks with a realistic motivation that does not reveal the interface path, and define success, assistance and error before the sessions (`RS-8`).
5. Plan the sample around the claim (`RS-10`) and state the limits of the planned sample.

### Write a guide

Keep the research question separate from the prompts. Write neutral prompts about specific episodes, follow-up prompts and the closing (`RS-2`). For a test, include the scenario script and the observation sheet.

### Synthesize research

1. Work only from the supplied records. Note which sessions or sources each observation comes from.
2. Keep three columns separate: what happened, interpretation, proposed response (`RS-3`).
3. Group observations into themes and trace each theme to sessions; preserve exceptions and disagreements.
4. For issues, record task, behavior, consequence, recovery, and how many participants were exposed and affected (`RS-9`). Do not convert small-study counts into prevalence.

## Required output

- The decision and question.
- The plan, guide or synthesis.
- Limits: coverage gaps, sample, method constraints.
- Changes to verify next.

## Stop conditions

- **HIL 1:** if the decision the research should inform is unknown and would change the method, propose two framings and ask the owner to choose.
- Before contacting participants or sending a survey, confirm authorization; the agent prepares materials, it does not recruit.

## Report

File paths, the method chosen and why, and what the output can and cannot support.
