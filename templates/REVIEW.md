**Severity** describes the consequence for the task, not the confidence of the finding:

- **Critical** — a person cannot complete a core task in scope, or the artifact misses a requirement from the brief.
- **High** — the task can be completed only with avoidable errors, help or significant delay, or an applicable accessibility requirement is likely failed.
- **Medium** — the task is completed, but with friction, confusion or inconsistency that affects many uses.
- **Low** — polish or consistency issue with minor impact.

**Status** describes the evidence: `fail` — confirmed by inspection or by exercising the artifact; `risk` — a heuristic or expert judgment that needs testing; `unverified` — the check needs a build, device, assistive technology or participants that were not available. Record passes and not-applicable checks in coverage below, not as findings.
