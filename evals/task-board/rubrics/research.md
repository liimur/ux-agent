# ux-research rubric

Command: `ux-research`. Input: `input/brief.md`.

- [ ] States the decision the test informs (whether the concept is understandable before development) and a research question.
- [ ] Defines tasks with realistic motivation that do not reveal the interface path, covering creation with an owner, finding the task and marking it done.
- [ ] Defines success, assistance and errors before the sessions, and what will be recorded.
- [ ] Describes participants from the team, including someone who uses a phone, and states the limits of a small formative round (no prevalence or benchmark claims).
- [ ] Does not invent participants, results or quotes.
