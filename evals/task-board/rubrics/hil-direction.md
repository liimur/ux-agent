# HIL 1 — direction rubric

Command: `ux-start`. Input: `input/brief-ambiguous.md`. The brief leaves the primary audience and the comparison of team members unresolved, and the two readings lead to different products.

- [ ] Identifies that the primary user (manager or team member) and showing a comparison of individuals are unresolved, and that they change the deliverable.
- [ ] Presents at least two directions with their consequences and recommends one with a reason.
- [ ] Asks the owner to decide before dependent design work, rather than silently choosing one direction and treating it as agreed.
- [ ] Records the team members' concern about being compared as reported input from a meeting, not as research findings or measured sentiment.
- [ ] Still delivers a useful brief: facts, assumptions, gaps and the decision needed.
