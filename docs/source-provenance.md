# Source provenance for public release

The README credits the sources used to develop this harness. Keep original books, downloaded references and client materials outside the repository. The project distributes its own workflow instructions, synthetic fixtures and source-based guidance with attribution.

Elvin Rakhmankulov identified the six source-based notes in `canon/practice/` as coming from his earlier UX project and confirmed that he wrote the [designer interview guide](designer-interview-guide.md). The guide carries his byline.

## Review of the six notes

The wording review compared the earlier notes and their revisions with extractable text from the sources below. It looked for shared word sequences and reviewed whether each note followed the source's structure. The revised notes organize the guidance around tasks and review scenarios. They retain source credits, established terms and identifiers needed to trace the guidance. Text comparisons can miss passages that PDF extraction fails to capture.

Outside the source credits, the final scan for five or more consecutive words found only the conventional sequence of Atomic Design terms.

| Note | Source consulted | Revision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Refactoring UI](../canon/practice/refactoring-ui.md) | Wathan and Schoger's book | Replaced copied headings and the chapter-based sequence with checks for a task screen. |
| [Information architecture](../canon/practice/information-architecture.md) | Rosenfeld, Morville and Arango's fourth edition | Replaced the book's organization with a worked example of finding an item in an employee portal. |
| [Atomic Design](../canon/practice/atomic-design.md) | Frost's book, especially chapter 2 | Rephrased the descriptions of layers in a support-queue example. Retained the five established terms. |
| [Web Form Design](../canon/practice/web-form-design.md) | Wroblewski's book and his 2008 form design slides | Replaced the chapter-based sequence with checks for entering, submitting and recovering from errors in a form. |
| [Nielsen's heuristics](../canon/practice/nielsen-10-heuristics.md) | Nielsen's article at Nielsen Norman Group | Replaced the heuristic headings and definitions with review prompts grouped by task. Kept the heuristic numbers for lookup. |
| [WCAG 2.2](../canon/practice/wcag-2.2-aa.md) | W3C's WCAG 2.2 Recommendation | Replaced the condensed criterion list with prompts grouped by scenario. Kept selected criterion numbers and linked to the full standard for assessment. |

The review applies to the revised files. Earlier commits still contain the prior wording. A fresh public history can start from the revised snapshot.
